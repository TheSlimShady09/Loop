The name

One loop of rope. One idea: come back for more.

LOOP started in a Canadian garage with a lasso knot and a heavyweight blank. We cut everything oversized, brush every fleece twice, and stitch a rope-loop label into the hem so you always know where it came from.

No seasonal churn. No throwaway fits. Just pieces made to be worn into the ground and bought again.
